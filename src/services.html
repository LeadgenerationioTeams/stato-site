What we build.

Five kinds of software, one way of working. Most projects combine two or three.

01

Custom web apps

Browser-based software built around how your business actually works.

  • Internal tools and dashboards
  • Booking and scheduling apps
  • Admin areas with role-based logins
02

Client portals

Give your customers or buyers their own login to see their data, and only theirs.

  • Multiple client accounts, each isolated
  • User roles and permissions
  • Set-password invite links
03

Lead-gen platforms

Forms, landing pages and lead routing for businesses that live on enquiries.

  • Multi-step forms and page builders
  • Consent capture with proof of wording
  • A/B testing of forms
04

CRMs & integrations

A CRM shaped to your pipeline, connected to the tools you already pay for.

  • Lead pipelines and job stages
  • Call tracking, SMS and WhatsApp
  • Accounting sync (e.g. Xero)
05

Automation & AI

Take repetitive work off your team with scheduled jobs, queues and AI where it genuinely helps.

  • Scheduled jobs and background queues
  • Automatic research and enrichment of leads
  • AI assistants over your own data (MCP)
