FAQs

Common questions.

Do you supply developers to join our team?

No. We don't hire out developers. We take on a project, design and build it, and can run it for you afterwards.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. Typical timelines are on the How we work page; we agree a plan after discovery rather than promising dates up front.

Who owns the code?

It depends on the project. On a custom project, you own the code we build for you. On a build-and-licence project, Stato keeps ownership and licenses the software to you.

What does it cost?

Pricing is custom and depends on the product. Every project is quoted after discovery.
